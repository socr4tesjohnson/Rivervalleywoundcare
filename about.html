About River Valley Wound Care

Dedicated to excellence in wound care through compassion, innovation, and expertise

Our Mission

To provide exceptional, patient-centered wound care that promotes healing, restores function, and improves quality of life.

At River Valley Wound Care, we believe that every patient deserves specialized attention and evidence-based treatment. Our multidisciplinary approach combines the latest medical advances with compassionate care to achieve optimal healing outcomes.

We understand that living with a chronic or non-healing wound can be challenging, both physically and emotionally. That's why we're committed to not just treating wounds, but supporting the whole person throughout their healing journey.

Our Values

  • Excellence: Committed to the highest standards of medical care
  • Compassion: Treating every patient with dignity and respect
  • Innovation: Utilizing cutting-edge treatments and technology
  • Collaboration: Working together with patients and providers
  • Integrity: Honest, ethical care you can trust

Our Treatment Approach

1. Comprehensive Assessment

Every patient receives a thorough evaluation including:

  • Detailed wound examination
  • Medical history review
  • Vascular assessment
  • Nutritional evaluation
  • Infection screening

2. Personalized Treatment Plan

We develop customized care strategies that address:

  • Underlying medical conditions
  • Wound-specific factors
  • Patient lifestyle and goals
  • Evidence-based interventions
  • Expected healing timeline

3. Advanced Treatment

Utilizing cutting-edge therapies including:

  • Specialized wound dressings
  • Debridement techniques
  • Growth factor applications
  • Compression therapy
  • Offloading devices

4. Ongoing Monitoring

Regular follow-up ensures optimal healing:

  • Progress documentation
  • Treatment adjustments
  • Complication prevention
  • Patient support and education
  • Outcome measurement
