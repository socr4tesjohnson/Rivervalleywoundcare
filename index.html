Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our wound care services

We treat a wide variety of wounds including diabetic ulcers, pressure ulcers, venous and arterial ulcers, surgical wounds, traumatic wounds, burns, and non-healing wounds. Our specialists are equipped to handle both acute and chronic wound conditions.

While many insurance plans require a referral from your primary care physician, some do not. We recommend checking with your insurance provider. Our office staff can also help verify your insurance benefits and referral requirements.

Please bring your insurance card, a valid photo ID, a list of current medications, any relevant medical records or test results, and information about your current wound care routine. If you have a referral from another physician, please bring that as well.

Initial consultations typically take 45-60 minutes as we conduct a comprehensive assessment. Follow-up appointments usually last 30-45 minutes depending on the treatment required. We strive to stay on schedule while ensuring each patient receives thorough care.

We accept most major insurance plans including Medicare, Medicaid, and most private insurances. Please contact our office to verify that we accept your specific insurance plan. Our staff is happy to help with insurance verification and pre-authorization if needed.

We offer state-of-the-art wound care treatments including debridement, bioengineered skin substitutes, negative pressure wound therapy, compression therapy, and advanced dressings. Our team stays current with the latest evidence-based wound care protocols.

Treatment frequency varies depending on your specific wound type and healing progress. Most patients are seen 1-2 times per week initially, with the frequency adjusted as healing progresses. Your care plan will be customized to your individual needs.

We prioritize patient comfort during all procedures. While some treatments may cause mild discomfort, we use various pain management techniques including local anesthesia when appropriate. Please communicate any pain concerns with our team so we can adjust your treatment accordingly.
