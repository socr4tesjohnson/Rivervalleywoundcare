Specialized Wound Care Services

Advanced treatments for all types of wounds

🩺

Diabetic Wound Care

Specialized treatment for diabetic foot ulcers and complications, focusing on prevention and healing.

  • Diabetic foot ulcer treatment
  • Neuropathy assessment
  • Vascular screening
  • Custom orthotics and offloading
  • Patient education on foot care
  • Infection management

Success Rate: 92% healing within 12 weeks

💺

Pressure Ulcer Treatment

Expert care for pressure injuries with advanced healing protocols and prevention strategies.

  • All stages of pressure injuries
  • Advanced wound dressings
  • Pressure redistribution devices
  • Nutritional optimization
  • Surgical consultation when needed
  • Caregiver education

Specialty: Stage 3 & 4 pressure injuries

🔄

Venous & Arterial Ulcers

Comprehensive vascular wound care addressing circulation and underlying disease processes.

  • Venous insufficiency treatment
  • Compression therapy management
  • Arterial disease evaluation
  • Vascular surgery coordination
  • Lymphedema management
  • Edema reduction protocols

Focus: Addressing root vascular causes

🔪

Surgical & Traumatic Wounds

Post-operative wound care and treatment for traumatic injuries requiring specialized attention.

  • Surgical site infection treatment
  • Dehiscence management
  • Trauma wound care
  • Complex laceration treatment
  • Scar management
  • Burn care (1st & 2nd degree)

Specialty: Complex surgical complications

🔬

Advanced Wound Therapies

Cutting-edge treatments for wounds that haven't responded to conventional care.

  • Negative pressure wound therapy (NPWT)
  • Bioengineered skin substitutes
  • Growth factor applications
  • Cellular and tissue-based products
  • Hyperbaric oxygen therapy referral
  • Platelet-rich plasma (PRP)

Technology: Latest FDA-approved treatments

🧬

Infection Management

Aggressive treatment of wound infections with advanced diagnostics and therapies.

  • Wound culture and sensitivity testing
  • Biofilm management
  • Antimicrobial dressing selection
  • IV antibiotic coordination
  • MRSA and resistant organism treatment
  • Infection prevention protocols

Expertise: Complex and resistant infections

Debridement Services
