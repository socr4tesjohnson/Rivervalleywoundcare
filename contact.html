Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a referral?

While referrals are not always required, some insurance plans do require them. We recommend checking with your insurance provider. We're happy to help coordinate with your primary care physician.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring your insurance cards, photo ID, list of current medications, and any relevant medical records or recent test results. If you have a referral, please bring that as well.

How long will treatment take?

Treatment duration varies depending on wound type, size, and individual healing factors. Most patients see significant improvement within 4-12 weeks. We'll provide a personalized timeline during your consultation.

Do you offer same-day appointments?

We strive to accommodate urgent cases as quickly as possible. Call us in the morning, and we'll do our best to see you the same day for acute wound concerns.

What if I need care outside of business hours?

For urgent concerns, call our main number. Our on-call physician will return your call promptly. For life-threatening emergencies, always call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room.
